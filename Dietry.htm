Healthy Living

Building a sustainable fitness routine comes down to managing three core pillars: progressive physical training, recovery, and consistent nutrition.Resistance & Strength Training: Target major muscle groups (legs, chest, back, core) 2 to 4 times per week using compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses, rows). Focus on progressive overload—gradually increasing weight, reps, or sets over time. Cardiovascular Conditioning: Combine Zone 2 cardio (steady-state aerobic exercise where you can maintain a conversation, 150 minutes/week) with high-intensity interval training (HIIT) once or twice weekly for heart health and athletic capacity. Mobility & Active Recovery: Prioritize dynamic stretching before workouts and static stretching or mobility drills post-workout to improve joint range of motion and prevent injury Drink alot of water daily to aviod dehydration
